Breaking News Bot

A personal desktop app that turns verified breaking news into short TikTok videos and photo slides, and uploads the videos to its owner's own TikTok account.

How it works

  1. Step 1Reads headlines from public news sites every 30 minutes.
  2. Step 2Picks only major stories and confirms them with several sources.
  3. Step 3Makes a video with photos, captions and narration, or a photo slideshow.
  4. Step 4Uploads the video to the owner's TikTok through TikTok's official Content Posting API.

TikTok integration

The app uses TikTok Login Kit so the owner can connect their own account, and the Content Posting API to send videos to their TikTok inbox as drafts or post them directly. Every post is labeled as AI-generated content. The app does not access other users' accounts or data.
