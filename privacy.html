Breaking News Bot

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

What the app is

Breaking News Bot is a desktop app that runs on its owner's own computer. It reads public news headlines, turns major stories into short videos and photo slides, and uploads the videos to the owner's own TikTok account.

Information the app uses

  • TikTok login details: when you connect TikTok, the app receives an access token, a refresh token and your TikTok account ID (open_id). These are needed to upload videos for you.
  • Your videos: the videos and captions the app creates are sent to TikTok when you choose to upload them.
  • Public news: headlines and articles from public news websites, and free-licensed photos from Wikimedia Commons, Openverse and Pexels.

Where it is stored

Everything stays on your own computer. The TikTok tokens are saved in a local file inside the app folder. The app has no server and no database, and the developer cannot see your data.

Who it is shared with

  • TikTok receives the videos and captions you upload, under TikTok's own privacy policy.
  • Anthropic (Claude) receives public news text to pick stories and write scripts. No TikTok data is sent to it.
  • Microsoft text-to-speech receives the narration script to create the voice.

Nothing is sold, and nothing is used for advertising.

Data about other people

The app does not collect or store information about other TikTok users. It only acts on the account of the person who connects it.

Removing your data

Delete the file data/tiktok_token.json in the app folder, or delete the app folder. You can also remove the app's access at any time in TikTok under Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services permissions.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date.

Breaking News Bot is a personal desktop app made by an individual developer. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by TikTok.
